About Me

Hey there! I'm Kiyazu, but you can call me Kazu. I'm a 17-year-old developer from Frankfurt, Germany, with a passion for creating and modifying digital experiences.

My journey in programming started with curiosity and has evolved into expertise across multiple languages. I specialize in game modding, particularly for Fortnite, where I combine creativity with technical skills to push boundaries and create unique experiences.

What I Do

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, watching anime, or working on personal projects that challenge my skills and expand my knowledge.

I spend my time wandering through lines of code, finding beauty in the chaos of development. There's something oddly comforting about debugging at 3 AM when the world feels empty and quiet.

My Work

I create modifications that breathe new life into existing games, particularly focusing on Fortnite modding. Each project is like painting with code, creating something that didn't exist before.

Sometimes I wonder if the digital worlds I create are more real than reality itself. But hey, that's probably just the caffeine talking.
